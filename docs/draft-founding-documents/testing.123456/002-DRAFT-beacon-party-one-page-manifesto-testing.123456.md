# The Beacon Party — One Page Manifesto (DRAFT)

**Steering Britain through the AI transformation: fairly, safely, honestly.**

## Why we exist

AI is already changing work. UK job vacancies have nearly halved since 2022, and hiring is falling fastest in the jobs AI can do. Seven in ten of us are worried about AI's economic impact, and only 7% believe its gains will be shared fairly. The government has said it is preparing for "unprecedented" job losses. Its main new measure for workers is a study.

**We think Britain deserves a plan, not a study.**

## What we believe

- AI is already a major force in our economy and society, and its influence will grow fast.
- The next few years will be a **bumpy road** for jobs, communities and our safety.
- If we navigate it well, AI can bring **shared abundance** for everyone, in every nation, and for the planet.

**We are not a tech party.** We don't exist to speed AI up or to stop it. The change is coming either way. Our job is to do the politics that gets Britain through it together. Hard work and talent should still be rewarded. Nobody should be left behind.

## Our Beacons

Beacons are destinations with a date and concrete steps. We publish progress on every one.

| Beacon | Year | Where we're heading |
|---|---|---|
| **Bridge** | 2028 | Anyone who loses their job to AI gets a **Transition Income** of most of their previous pay for up to 18 months, with help to retrain and protection for their home. |
| **UK AI Stake** | 2030 | Invest where Britain can win (AI safety, health, finance, creative industries), with **public equity** in return. Cheaper clean power for AI. At least £1 in £3 of public AI spending goes to UK firms. |
| **Safe AI** | 2030 | Binding safety rules for the most powerful AI. Statutory powers for the AI Security Institute. Global standards led from Britain. Deepfake-free elections. |
| **Global Treaty** | 2032 | Britain won't go it alone where that would drive investment away. We will build a binding international treaty on a minimum AI tax, shared safety standards and a global dividend, starting with the UK's 2027 G20 presidency. |
| **Shared Abundance Fund** | 2032 | A national fund, built from AI equity stakes and windfall levies, pays a **dividend to every adult**. |
| **Universal High Income** | 2038 | As AI does more of the work, a guaranteed income floor rises towards a comfortable standard of living for all. |

## Our promise: no quiet U-turns

Every Beacon has a target, a date and a named owner, tracked publicly and checked independently. If the facts change, we will **change the Beacon openly**. We will say what changed and why the new course makes sense. We won't let a promise quietly disappear.

## Governing everything else

AI will touch almost everything: the NHS, schools, housing, defence, the cost of living. We will bring in experienced people from across politics, public service and business to run these well, and we will keep the public finances sound.

## Vote Beacon if…

…you can see AI changing your job, your children's prospects or your town. You want a government that plans for it, shares the gains, and tells you the truth along the way.
