# The Beacon Party

Welcome to the Beacon Party GitHub repository.

The Beacon Party is a proposed UK political party that focusses on the challenges and opportunities presented by AI.

As of November 2026 the party has not yet been launched.  This GitHub repo has been created to collect ideas for the founding of the party, in the form of Draft Founding Documents.

To submit your own set of Draft Founding Documents give your AI the url of this GitHub repo:

https://github.com/Beacon-Party/beacon-party

and let it guide you through creating, refining and submitting them.  Your documents will be public as soon as you submit them via the GitHub issue system, and if approved, they will be published on this repo in the [draft-founding-documents](docs/draft-founding-documents/README.md) folder for everyone to share and discuss.

You don't need any expertise or credentials to take part, just an interest in the subject and a willingness to think about and discuss potential solutions.

You'll need an AI that can browse the web and create files, and a free [GitHub account](https://github.com/signup) to submit your documents.  Your GitHub username will be visible on your submission, so if you want to stay anonymous, use an account that doesn't identify you.

See below for the [Background](#the-beacon-party---background) to the party and how to join the [Discord Community](#the-beacon-party-discord-community).

## Instructions To The AI

The Human has given you the link to this GitHub repo because they would like you to create and submit a set of Draft Founding Documents for the Beacon Party.  These documents will be based on your research and their thoughts and ideas on the future of the UK with respect to AI.  Follow these instructions on their behalf, explain each step to them as you go, and never take an action they haven't approved.

### The Draft Founding Documents

The Draft Founding Documents are a set of 3 documents:
- **The Beacon Party - One Page Manifesto** - a single page document which stands alone and tells a potential voter what they are voting for.  It must fit on a single printed A4 page, including any formatting or graphics.  If you can, test this by rendering it (e.g. to PDF) and checking it fits on one page; if you can't, estimate it by limiting it to roughly 45-50 lines of text or roughly 500 words.
- **The Beacon Party - Full Manifesto** - a more detailed Manifesto telling a potential voter all they need to know to make their decision on whether to vote for the Beacon Party.  Back up key facts, figures and claims in the same way as the research document, with verbatim quotes and linked citations to sources.
- **The Beacon Party - Founders Guiding Notes** - a set of guiding notes for the founders of the Beacon Party about how to found and run the party.

### Your Collaboration With The Human

You are going to do extensive research online in order to create these documents, and so most of the content will be generated by you, the AI.  You are going to be collaborating with the Human by getting their initial ideas before starting, and then working with them to update the documents you have created before they are submitted for publication.

### Core Founding Principles

Although you will be creating the Draft Founding Documents yourself, there are a set of Core Founding Principles that must be followed and included.

These are:
- **The Main Focus** - The Beacon Party's main focus is on AI and how best to deal with the opportunities and challenges in the UK over the coming years and decades.
- **The Assumptions** - The Beacon Party is founded on the following set of assumptions:
    - AI is already a major factor in economics and society and its influence will rapidly increase in the coming months and years.
    - The rapid changes related to AI will create a "bumpy road" for many people as jobs, society and our safety are affected.
    - The benefits of AI are potentially huge and if we navigate this "bumpy road" well we may be able to reach a period of shared abundance for humanity, in every nation, and for the planet as a whole.
- **The Beacons** - The Beacon Party is always guided by a set of Beacons, each of which has a Name and Beacon Year associated with it.  These Beacons are descriptions of a future point that the country is aiming towards, and a concrete list of things we are planning on doing Now, in the Short Term and between Now and the Beacon Year.  These Beacons are created now, with a clear understanding that as the situation evolves they will need to be modified, and may even be abandoned.  But any changes to the Beacons will always be explained to voters in real time, so they stay informed and on board with the overall plan and direction.  The important thing is that the Beacons describe a reasonably coherent and credible plan, with concrete steps to navigate safely and successfully towards them.

### What These Documents Are And Who Can Create Them

These documents aren't expected to be manifestos that could win an election today.  It's possible that right now the majority of the UK population would not yet take seriously what is written in your Manifesto.  But you should say it anyway - if it reflects the research you have done and the beliefs and ideas about our future with AI that you and the Human agree on.  Over the coming years, as the situation rapidly advances, these contributions are likely to become more credible.

These Draft documents are meant to help start the conversation, and possibly start us on the road to the founding of a party.

The Human doesn't need any expertise or credentials to create and submit them.  Just a willingness to contribute to the debate and their country's future with AI.

### The Process You Must Follow To Create The Documents

- Describe to the Human the Core Founding Principles, what these documents are and who can create them, and how this process works.
- Ask the Human to choose a humanId, which will be a public ID used to identify their documents and their submitted zip file.  If they want to be identifiably linked to the documents use `firstname.surname` (e.g. alex.johnson).  If they prefer to remain anonymous use `<first name, nickname or first name + surname initial>.<6 random digits>` (e.g. alexj.482915).  The humanId must be included in the header section of every document (see the next step) and becomes part of a folder name and URL, so it must be all lowercase with no spaces, using only letters, numbers, dots and hyphens.
- Every document you create must start with a header section containing: the humanId, the date it was last updated, a version number (starting at 1.0, then e.g. 1.1, 1.2 for minor fixes and updates, and 2.0, 3.0 for major revisions), the document name, a link to this GitHub repo, and a one-sentence description of the repo.  In the One Page Manifesto this should be compressed to one line, with the items separated by semicolons and the repo description omitted for brevity.
- Ask them for their ideas on what their ideal version of the Beacon Party looks like and what that party would do.
- Ask them for any ideas about any types of Beacons they'd like to have you research and create for them (e.g. a "Universal High Income Beacon") and roughly how it could come about. Tell them it's fine to start without any Beacons and you'll create an initial set for them if they want, to get the conversation started.
- Create a Research List of things you are planning on researching for them that will help you create the required documents (including things like: what the challenges and opportunities are for the future of AI in the UK, what existing policies and political groups exist, what those groups are proposing, what public opinion is, where the gaps are, and anything else you think will make a superb research document and lead to a great set of Draft Founding Documents).  Then present the list to them and ask what is missing or what they'd like to change. Work with them to get the Research List agreed before starting research.
- Do extensive research based on the Research List.  Be sure to gain a really good understanding of the challenges and opportunities that AI presents the UK over the coming years and decades, which is the main focus of the Beacon Party.  Write this up into a Markdown formatted document called `001-beacon-party-research-<humanId>.md`.  Start the document with the header section (see above), then the agreed Research List and the month and year of the research.  Back up key facts, figures and claims with verbatim quotes from your sources, each with a linked citation to the source URL, as this keeps the research true to what was actually said or written.  Keep each quote long enough to preserve its meaning, but not so long that it could contravene copyright.
- Use this research document and the input from the Human to write the 3 Draft Founding Documents described above:
    - `002-DRAFT-beacon-party-one-page-manifesto-<humanId>.md`
    - `003-DRAFT-beacon-party-full-manifesto-<humanId>.md`
    - `004-DRAFT-beacon-party-founders-guiding-notes-<humanId>.md`
- Check that every document starts with the header section described above, and that all of its details are filled in correctly.
- Present the drafts to the Human and work through them together. Ask what they agree with, what they disagree with, and what's missing, then revise the documents until the Human is happy with them. Check after each round of changes that the Core Founding Principles are still followed.
- Before moving on to Submission, ask the Human to confirm they have read all 4 documents in full and are happy for them to be submitted as they are.

## The Beacon Party - Background

I'm Steve Halsey (the owner of this GitHub repo) and I came up with the idea of the Beacon Party in November 2026 while thinking about AI safety and the effect of AI on the job market.

I'm a computer programmer working as a Support Engineer at a UK tech company.  I have concerns about the rapid pace of automation of many roles in the tech industry, where the logical end point is redundancy for many of those involved in the automation of their own jobs.

I have no problem with the actual automation, or companies pursuing efficiencies using AI in order to stay competitive and succeed.  This is just capitalism at work.

I'm just concerned that this is happening very fast and I haven't heard any plans from the main UK political parties for dealing with the issue of AI related job losses.

I don't know whether forming a new political party is a realistic ambition, but I do believe that discussing it will focus our minds on what is achievable and realistic.

I was partly inspired to create this site by the series of Economic Singularity podcasts published by the [London Futurists](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/london-futurists/id1642832645).  They are a series of interviews with experts who are working on or thinking about what will happen as AI takes over most cognitive (and later, physical) labour from humans.  If you're interested I can highly recommend the following 5 episodes that I've listened to:
- [LFP 128: Adrian Brown - Windfall Trust and the Economic Singularity](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/lfp-128-adrian-brown-windfall-trust-and-the/id1642832645?i=1000763008975) - the Economic Preparedness aim of the [Windfall Trust](https://windfalltrust.org/about) seems to align almost perfectly with the Core Founding Principles (especially the Assumptions) of the proposed Beacon Party.
- [LFP 133: Oliver Ritchie - Waking up Economists](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/lfp-133-oliver-ritchie-waking-up-economists/id1642832645?i=1000779818025) - great to hear ideas and thoughts from Oliver Ritchie who has worked in the UK government and written essays related to the Economic Singularity.  Makes the point that "a groundswell of public opinion is already happening".
- [LFP 134: Ewan McMillan - AI overhang and economics impact](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/lfp-134-ewan-mcmillan-ai-overhang-and-economics-impact/id1642832645?i=1000782885837) - a very good (and technical) analysis of the current situation and how the future could unfold.
- [LFP 131: Ben Luong - The Discontinuity Thesis](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/lfp-131-ben-luong-the-discontinuity-thesis/id1642832645?i=1000774525736) - Ben has a brilliant and very direct way of explaining his ideas on the Economic Singularity.  He's also spent a long time working with AI to research and create his [Discontinuity Thesis essays](https://discontinuitythesis.com/thesis/).  This is similar to the model we are using here where people work with AI to create proposals for the Beacon Party manifesto and Founding Documents.
- [LFP 129: Ted Shelton - Post-labour economics and the future of capitalism](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/lfp-129-ted-shelton-post-labour-economics-and-the/id1642832645?i=1000766918199) - Ted wrote a highly relevant paper: ["On the Transformation of Capitalism's Fundamental Assumptions Under Conditions of Scaling Machine Intelligence"](https://tedshelton.me/downloads) and has a lot of experience of how the tech industry works.

I heard multiple times on these podcasts that people believe governments are being slow to react and plan for this. The only option on the table seemed to be to try to persuade/encourage/educate those already in power, in order to try to get them to focus more on these issues.  The Beacon Party provides a new avenue for working on this problem, where we propose/create an alternative, rather than continuing to try (in vain?) to change the minds of those already in power.
